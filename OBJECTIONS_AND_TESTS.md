# Standard objections and where they are tested

Reviewers of this repository tend to raise a recurring set of objections. Several are built into the study as falsification tests; others mark declared open points. This page indexes both, with the place where each point is tested or stated. The list is not exhaustive. Rows whose test lives in this repository carry a path in backticks and can be recomputed here; rows citing only §-numbers point into the manuscript.

Reading note: the 50-page empirical manuscript is not part of this repository; it is available from the author. It carries the within-substrate correlation evidence, the falsification section (§8), and the substrate chapters cited below.

Reference state: empirical manuscript v5 (build of 12 Aug 2026) · formal framework, 30 Jul 2026 · repository snapshot policy in `README.md` (Reference state).

## Objections that are built in as tests

| # | Objection | Where it is tested | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normalized cumulative monotone series fit sigmoids almost regardless of input; high R² proves nothing. | Manuscript §8.2 (Layer 1b) raises this objection itself; `data/model_comparison/`. | On random monotone curves the two-parameter power law dominates and the four-parameter sigmoid is never the winning class; on the empirical substrates the logistic wins 6 of 8 (`data/model_comparison/README.md`). The criterion is the class comparison with complexity penalized, and the inflection-outcome relation holds across all four model families (§8.2). |
| 2 | If R² is not the evidence, what is? | Manuscript §4.12 reading rule: "the load-bearing statement is the within-substrate correlation, not the absolute value". | Timber: r(â, fracture stress) = -0.83, n = 230 (§4.1). LLM conversations: rho(â, annotated failure) = -0.359 cluster-robust, n = 202 (§4.10). A noise process carries no independently measured failure quantity to correlate with. |
| 3 | AR(1)/random-walk nulls are too weak; surrogates preserving each substrate's marginals are required. | Manuscript §1.2 (demarcation), §4.3, §4.7, §4.10, §4.11, §8. | Free-ordering substrates carry such surrogates: turn-order destruction (conversations), note-order shuffle (music), a null with identical event statistics (earthquakes), length-matched calm phases (finance: R² 0.48 against 0.97 in crashes). For forced-ordering substrates (tensile test) a path surrogate is physically meaningless; there the test is the model comparison of row 1. |
| 4 | Points along a cumulative curve are autocorrelated; pooled inference overstates confidence. | Methods paper arXiv:2604.14414 (public); manuscript §3.3; `STATISTICS.md`. | The autocorrelation is treated as loading-history signal, and inference is cluster-robust with the specimen as the unit of analysis. The methods paper quantifies the overstatement of pooled inference; its title carries the 42% figure. |
| 5 | Ten substrates and many statistics invite a look-elsewhere effect. | `analysis/bonferroni_gamma_m.py`; `STATISTICS.md`; manuscript §1.4, §4.13. | Reporting is Bonferroni-conservative under the safety factor γM = α/9; the count recomputes here. The per-substrate status table separates conformant results from nominal-with-named-caveat (§4.13). |
| 6 | The music shuffle control keeps high R² and visible genre differences. | Manuscript §4.11; `data/musik_companion/`. | The pre-registered falsification condition is destruction of the genre monotonicity, and it occurs (Mann-Kendall n.s.; â clusters at about 0.27-0.34). ## Open by declaration

Stated as open in the documents themselves and kept open here, each with its named test path:

- The empirical premise of the second no-go, that a class with self-defeating reception is non-empty; a controlled mode-switch experiment would test it (formal framework, Remark 10.4).
- The Goldilocks principle T3: interior balance is proved, the placement near criticality is an empirical prediction (formal framework, §7).
- The pathwise specimen inflection: the transition theorem is stated at the level of the expected trajectory (formal framework, Theorem 2.2).
- The general-asymmetry statement behind the no-go: one certified direction suffices for non-universality; the general case is an open conjecture (formal framework, Remark 9.6).
- The cognition bridge: a theoretical extension with a defined longitudinal falsification path (manuscript §7.1).
- CRC survival coupling (manuscript §4.9) and battery end-of-life (EOL) coupling (manuscript §4.8).
- Independent cross-group replication: two structured programs, causal intervention and multi-agent symmetry sweep (manuscript §9.2).
